What internship opportunities are available for students at Howard University?

Howard University offers a diverse array of internship opportunities across various fields, leveraging its strong connections with local, national, and international organizations. Students can access internships through the university's Career and Alumni Services, which provides resources such as job fairs, networking events, and career counseling. Popular internship fields include business, healthcare, education, public policy, and the arts, with partnerships established with companies and institutions like the Smithsonian, NASA, and various government agencies.

Additionally, Howard encourages students to seek internships that align with their career aspirations, providing guidance on application processes and resume building. The university's emphasis on experiential learning means students can often receive academic credit for internships, allowing them to gain practical experience while fulfilling degree requirements. Overall, Howard's robust support system and strong alumni network significantly enhance students' internship prospects and overall career readiness.
